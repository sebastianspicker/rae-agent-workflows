# CLM-002 Diataxis Separation

## Claim statement

Documentation remains more maintainable when tutorial, how-to, reference, and
explanation surfaces are kept distinct.

## Claim class

`engineering_heuristic`

## Proof mode

Structural argument supported by documentation architecture doctrine and
operational maintainability evidence from the RAE corpus.

## Assumptions

- maintainability is measured over the full authorship cycle, not just initial
  writing
- the four Diátaxis quadrants map faithfully to the RAE corpus (tutorials,
  how-tos, reference docs, explanation science)
- authoring discipline is enforceable via tooling and review policy

None required for an engineering heuristic operating at documentation
architecture scope.

## Counterarguments

- small corpora may not benefit from strict quadrant separation
- disciplined authoring overhead may slow first-draft production

## Validity threats

- the heuristic applies to author burden; reader experience gains are less
  directly measured
- cross-quadrant pages can appear necessary when topics straddle concerns

## Review status

Adopted as a scoped documentation architecture heuristic.
